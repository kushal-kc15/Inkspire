FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Is InkSpire free to use?

Yes! Creating an account, reading stories, and publishing your own content is completely free. No hidden fees or paywalls.

Who can write on InkSpire?

Anyone with a story to tell. Whether you’re a seasoned journalist, a hobbyist blogger, or writing for the first time — you’re welcome here.

What topics can I write about?

Anything that adds value. Technology, lifestyle, personal growth, travel, creative fiction, tutorials — we have categories for all of it.

How do I grow my audience?

Write consistently, engage with the community, and use relevant categories. Our trending algorithm surfaces quality content to readers interested in your topics.

Can I import my posts from another platform?

We’re working on import tools. For now, you can copy and paste your content into our editor which preserves formatting nicely.

Is there a mobile app?

InkSpire is fully responsive and works beautifully on mobile browsers. A dedicated app is on our roadmap for the future.
